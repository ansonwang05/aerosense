# Aerosense

Historical ambient air quality records in India span nearly three decades but suffer from non-uniform monitoring networks and high missingness in modern metrics like PM 
2.5. This project establishes a robust data-cleaning and station-aggregation pipeline for historical pollutants (RSPM, SO 
2, NO2), characterizes long-term regional and seasonal variance, and benchmarks time-series predictive models against baseline lag methods to forecast recurring seasonal pollution peaks

Context & Challenge: Ambient pollution is severe and seasonal, but legacy data contains structural gaps (e.g., transition from SPM to RSPM, lack of early PM2.5)

Objective: Clean and analyze historical concentrations of SO2,NO2, and RSPM (1987–2015) to evaluate regional disparities and identify correlations with environmental regulations.

Deliverable: Multi-year trend dashboards, policy timeline overlays, and an initial lag-based forecasting baseline.
